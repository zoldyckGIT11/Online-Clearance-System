Need to know

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers for students starting their online clearance.

Which files can I upload?

You can upload PDF, Word, JPG, and PNG files for each office requirement.

Can I change my selected offices?

You can cancel unfinished office requests and submit a new selection while your clearance is still in progress.

How will I know when an office responds?

Your dashboard shows the latest status, and the portal sends in-app notifications for important updates.

When is my clearance complete?

Your clearance is completed automatically once every active office has approved its requirement.
